👋 Welcome to the Productivity Lab

You're one week into the accelerated program. Your professors and your internship supervisor have been emailing — and your calendar is still empty. Turn that inbox into a week you can actually execute.

1
Sort your inboxCreate four folders — one per class, one for your internship — and drag each email where it belongs. Organize first, act second.
2
Extract the tasksOpen each filed email and decide: does someone need something from you, or is it just context? Real asks become tasks in your Braindump.
3
Verify your BraindumpEvery task starts locked. Confirm a realistic time estimate and priority — the emails tell you what's expected — and it unlocks for scheduling.
4
Block your weekDrag tasks into morning, afternoon, and evening blocks around your locked class sessions. Big projects come pre-chunked — spread the parts out in order.
5
Earn Orientation PointsGuardrails watch your study quota, deadlines, and burnout risk. Master them to earn ⭐ OP and badges toward your end-of-orientation award.
